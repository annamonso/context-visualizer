ChronoLog Observability, System Architecture

A workflow observability and provenance system. Heterogeneous sources at the top, a single write pipeline on the left, one shared store at the bottom, and three consumers on the right. Teal is the write and storage side, amber is the read and consume side. Every layer has interchangeable, decoupled backends.

How to read it. Interchangeable sources sit at the top. Data falls down the single write pipeline on the left, Capture then Publish then Transport then Persist, where each box is one isolated job and a darker box means deeper in the pipeline. The pipeline writes down into the Shared Provenance Store at the bottom (teal arrow). The three tall consumer cards on the right, the ChronoDoctor AI plus analysis, the Query API, and the live Dashboards, read back up from that store (amber arrows). Swappable backends appear at every layer: SSE, HTTP or collector for transport; ChronoLog, JSONL or CSV for storage; heuristic or Claude for the diagnoser; ChronoLog or custom for the read adapter. The design goal is modularity, any backend can be replaced without touching the others.
